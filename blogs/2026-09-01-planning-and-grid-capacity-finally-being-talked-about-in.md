The UK Government’s new National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF) was published last week, setting out the Government’s policies for planning and decision-making on development proposals in England. With a whole section of the policy dedicated to ‘Securing clean energy and water’, this was welcome news from a network capacity perspective. It sets out its priorities for the benefits of renewables and low-carbon energy sources, while effectively forcing local planning to actively account for local network capacity and grid connection requirements.
