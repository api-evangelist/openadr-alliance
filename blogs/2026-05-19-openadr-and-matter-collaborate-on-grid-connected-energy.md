Last week we announced a liaison agreement with the Connectivity Standards Alliance, which manages the smart home standard, Matter to help drive the adoption of grid-connected residential energy management. This is an important industry collaboration that will help address the need for seamless communication between smart home devices and the energy grid. Members of our two organizations will work to make it easier to turn connected homes into active participants in demand response (DR) programs.
