Meet the Board Blog Series: Mary Ann Piette, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory The OpenADR Alliance is pleased to introduce Mary Ann Piette, Associate Laboratory Director for Energy Technologies at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory and lead of the California Load Flexibility Research and Deployment Hub (CalFlexHub). In her role, Mary Ann manages the development of affordable and secure energy systems. Over the last five years, CalFlexHub has demonstrated advanced load-flexible technologies and developed key elements for OpenADR 3.
