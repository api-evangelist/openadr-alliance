It has been nearly two years since the OpenADR Alliance exhibited at the Power+Utilities event in Melbourne. That event was a significant milestone in educating attendees on the OpenADR protocol and included Terry Paddy, Managing Director of member company Cortexo, who introduced the New Zealand FlexTalk project. Cortexo, a New Zealand-based energy solutions provider with 20 years of experience in remote control and monitoring, has been a key player in this space.
